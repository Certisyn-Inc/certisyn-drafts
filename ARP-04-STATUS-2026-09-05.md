## What actually blocks the freeze

Stage A is otherwise complete. Two decisions hold the `>>> FREEZE THE DRAFT
SOURCE <<<` line, and both came from people already inside the interop group.

**A9. Digest-suite transition.** Nenad Vasic's item 2. Records under a
predecessor suite stay valid at their recorded positions, while a retroactive
re-digest of the same bytes under the successor must refuse. His
discriminating property: a naive engine that re-hashes history under the new
suite agrees with the forged digest and accepts. Sits between 4.21 and 4.22,
and 4.22 currently says the chain is unbroken across a rotation without
saying what a re-digest of historical bytes must do. The decision is binary:
a Divergence Axis, or an explicit statement that suite transition belongs to
the verifier contract.

**A10. Two-clock skew on response freshness.** Emek Can Dogru's question,
raised as a question rather than a defect. ARP's response freshness tolerance
is decided across two clocks, the responding service stamping `response-time`
and the reader comparing against its own, and neither 6.4.3 nor item 26 names
them or bounds skew. `-03` uses neither the word clock nor skew. The plan
allows ten minutes for it.

A5, the Document History "Since -03" paragraph, depends on A4, A6, A9 and
A10, so it cannot be written until those two land.

## One discrepancy to resolve before the freeze

`ARP-04.md` section 2.9.2, the Source-Data Version Identifier Set, is still
headed **"BLOCKING, and now urgent"** and lists three verified defects: no
ordering rule, a disjunctive state identifier with no discriminator, and the
value carried twice with no equality rule.

Your message to the SCITT list of 20 August states all three as repaired:
"Three things were wrong with the encoding, all found yesterday, all now
repaired", followed by the sorted Set, the explicit form discriminator with
the digest taken over the octets the publisher serves before decompression,
and the equality rule.

Either the document is stale or the message was ahead of the text. That has
to be settled before the freeze, because the public statement is the one
Sirkkavaara and Etcheverry are relying on: both said on 20 August that they
do not have this component and would have to add the field, and Sirkkavaara
proposed the shared substrate cite components normatively rather than
restate them. The component about to be lifted into shared text is the one
the sweep found three defects in.
